1.Your files stay on your phone

We do NOT collect, upload, store, share or sell your PDFs or images. All processing happens on your device: Images to PDF, Merge PDF, Split PDF, Protect PDF, Compress KB (images and PDF), PDF to Images, Image Resizer, Format Convert and OCR Text. The app works fully offline and requests no Internet access.

2.No account needed

We do NOT ask for an account, email address, phone number or password. No signup is required to use the app.

3.Permissions

We request only the permissions needed for the feature you use: Photos / Media (to pick the images you choose), Notifications (optional — to tell you when a file is ready), and Vibrate (haptic feedback when you slide to convert). The app does not request Camera or Internet access. You can deny a permission — that feature will not work, but the rest of the app will.

4.OCR

Text recognition (OCR) runs on-device. Text from your documents is not sent to any server by this app.

5.Ads and payments

Version 1.2 shows no ads. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no paid features. No payment details are collected.

6.Screenshot and screen recording prevention

The app prevents screenshots and screen recording inside the app. This is always on and cannot be turned off, so your documents cannot be captured by another app or by a screen recording while you use Chami. This protection applies only inside the app; it does not change anything outside the app.

7.Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8.Data deletion

Because your files stay on your device, deleting the app deletes all your app data. There is no account or server copy to delete.

9.Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above.

10.Contact

For privacy questions, email us at chami.support@gmail.com.
